# Foreign Ads Launch Plan

## Goal

Send paid and organic traffic to dedicated landing pages instead of the general home page.

## Landing Pages

- Seoul search intent: `/tattoo-in-seoul`
- Korea trip planning intent: `/korea-trip-tattoo`
- Structured request form: `/booking`

## Google Search Campaigns

### Campaign 1: Seoul now

Landing page:

```text
https://ethnichouseseoul.com/tattoo-in-seoul?utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=tattoo_in_seoul
```

Keyword examples:

- tattoo in Seoul
- Seoul tattoo booking
- English speaking tattoo artist Seoul
- fine line tattoo Seoul
- small tattoo Seoul
- Korea tattoo for foreigners

### Campaign 2: Korea trip planning

Landing page:

```text
https://ethnichouseseoul.com/korea-trip-tattoo?utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=korea_trip_tattoo
```

Keyword examples:

- Korea trip tattoo
- tattoo in Korea
- Seoul tattoo for foreigners
- small tattoo Korea trip
- Korean lettering tattoo Seoul

## Meta / TikTok Creative Angles

- A small tattoo for your Korea trip
- English-friendly tattoo booking in Seoul
- Before you leave Korea, take home a small memory
- Fine-line and lettering tattoos in Seoul

## First Test Budget

- Google Search: KRW 300,000
- Instagram: KRW 200,000
- Organic short-form content: 10 posts before scaling

## Metrics

- Landing page visits
- Booking form clicks
- Instagram DM clicks
- Real Instagram DM inquiries
- Confirmed bookings
- Country and language of each inquiry
