# Legal And Risk Notes

This file is an operating checklist, not legal advice.

## Platform Role

Seoul Ink Booking is positioned as a booking support and artist matching service.

The actual tattoo procedure, hygiene management, consent, customer service delivery, and legal compliance are handled by the individual artist or partner studio.

## Required Before Paid Traffic

- Review Korean tattoo-related legal exposure with a qualified professional.
- Confirm partner artist/studio responsibilities in writing.
- Prepare cancellation and deposit policy.
- Prepare customer consent wording.
- Keep the privacy notice updated.
- Avoid claims such as guaranteed safety, guaranteed medical outcomes, or official licensing status unless verified.

## Partner Checks

Before adding partner artists or studios, collect:

- Portfolio links
- Style and starting prices
- Booking availability process
- Cancellation policy
- Language support capability
- Hygiene and aftercare process
- Permission to use portfolio images and reviews
